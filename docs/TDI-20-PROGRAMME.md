# TDI-20.x — Convergence of an Autonomous Discovery Ecosystem

Status: **active Stage 0 bootstrap; not frozen; no confirmatory execution authorised**.

## Conjecture

A modular discovery loop that separates hypothesis generation, experiment, falsification/proof, algorithm optimization, deployment and observation can improve a declared frontier of knowledge quality over repeated cycles when proposer diversity, validator independence, provenance and retention of negative evidence are enforced.

## Primary null

Any apparent improvement is explained by benchmark leakage, evaluator overfitting, repeated selection on noise, increased compute or human steering; the loop does not improve held-out knowledge quality relative to simpler baselines.

## Stage map

- **TDI-20.0** — freeze object model, allowed component interfaces, knowledge-quality frontier, cycle budget, leakage boundaries and stop rules.
- **TDI-20.1** — deterministic toy discovery worlds with known hypotheses, counterexamples and proof/verification oracles.
- **TDI-20.2** — compare modular loop against proposal-only, test-only, optimize-only and monolithic self-judging baselines.
- **TDI-20.3** — introduce noisy proposers/validators and measure false promotion, forgetting and recovery.
- **TDI-20.4** — adapter-level non-final ecosystem trial using SciRust/SDE, ProofLab, Forge and other Memorithm components if justified.
- **TDI-20.5** — separately gated replication on a distinct problem family.

## Required controls

Equal total compute, hidden final truth, proposer/validator separation, no shared final labels, random-search and monolithic-agent baselines, ablations of provenance/negative-result retention, cycle-count controls and repeated-selection correction.

## Decision principle

Support requires improvement of a preregistered multi-objective frontier such as held-out validity, generality, experiment cost and false-promotion rate across cycles. Producing more artifacts or higher proposer confidence is not progress.

## Ecosystem boundary

SciRust/SDE may plan and represent research objects; ProofLab may provide formal proof authority; Forge may search executable algorithms; KVLab/TDI/other benches may provide experiments; NNIS/FLAT/ElasticXxx may execute or adapt; CCOS may provide replay/provenance. TDI-20 owns only the scientific evaluation of the combined loop.

## Non-claims

TDI-20 does not claim AGI, guaranteed self-improvement, universal convergence, autonomous scientific correctness or that any current Memorithm component already satisfies the conjecture.
