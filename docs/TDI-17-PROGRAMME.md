# TDI-17.x — Pre-Hallucinatory Representation Divergence

Status: **active Stage 0 bootstrap; not frozen; no confirmatory execution authorised**.

## Conjecture

Unsupported generation is sometimes preceded by a measurable divergence between three prospective quantities: evaluator-visible evidence, memory/state that is actually recoverable by the model, and the model's predictive trajectory. A divergence observable may therefore predict and causally localize some failures before the unsupported assertion is emitted.

## Primary null

Divergence observables add no predictive or intervention value beyond competent token-probability, entropy, retrieval-disagreement and task-difficulty controls.

## Stage map

- **TDI-17.0** — freeze evidence/recoverability/predictive-state definitions, prospective timing and non-leakage rules.
- **TDI-17.1** — extend deterministic controlled worlds from TDI-11 without touching protected final material.
- **TDI-17.2** — instrument development trajectories through explicit KV/state adapters.
- **TDI-17.3** — causal interventions on memory recoverability and matched sham interventions.
- **TDI-17.4** — compare divergence families against TDI-11-compatible risk baselines.

## Required controls

Entropy/margin, retrieval disagreement, visible-context support score, task depth, random/sham memory perturbations, post-hoc-only signals separated from prospective signals, and architecture-specific availability reporting.

## Decision principle

Support requires prospective held-out risk stratification plus an intervention result showing that changing recoverability changes the targeted failure rate under matched resource accounting. Prediction alone is not causal evidence.

## Ecosystem boundary

TDI-11 owns hallucination taxonomy and controlled-world truth. KVLab may provide KV-state instrumentation; CCOS may provide replay/causal-memory adapters. TDI-17 must not alter or consume TDI-11 protected final holdouts.

## Non-claims

TDI-17 does not claim one universal hallucination cause, a universal hidden-state detector, or that every unsupported statement is a memory failure.
