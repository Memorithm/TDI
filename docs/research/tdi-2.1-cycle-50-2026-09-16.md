# TDI-2.1 cycle-50 status — 2026-09-16

## Scope

This document closes the 50-slice autonomous TDI-2.1 cycle dedicated to experience-conditioned intuition and template transfer.

The scientific working definition remains:

- experience is accumulated and consolidated;
- reusable Boolean/relational templates represent transferable structure;
- a template may be matched and adapted to a novel situation;
- empirical reliability/support remain numeric rather than Boolean;
- insufficient experience is a first-class abstention outcome;
- latency is measured externally as a possible consequence of experience and is not an input to the intuition algorithm.

No historical TDI-2 result is modified.

## Scientific boundaries

- no PrimaryHoldout or protected/final population has been opened;
- no scientific claim that TDI-2.1 constitutes human-like intuition is made;
