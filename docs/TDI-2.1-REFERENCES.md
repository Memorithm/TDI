# TDI-2.1 — Reference register

Date frozen: 2026-09-16
Purpose: comparison context for the TDI-2.1 intuition programme.

## Associative memory

1. Saul Santos, Vlad Niculae, Daniel McNamee, André F. T. Martins. *Hopfield-Fenchel-Young Networks: A Unified Framework for Associative Memory Retrieval*. arXiv:2411.08590, 2024. https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.08590
   - Relevant to generalized energy functions, sparse retrieval and structured associative memory.

2. Dennis Wu, Jerry Yao-Chieh Hu, Teng-Yun Hsiao, Han Liu. *Uniform Memory Retrieval with Larger Capacity for Modern Hopfield Models*. arXiv:2404.03827, 2024. https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.03827
   - Relevant to learned similarity maps, metastability and retrieval robustness.

3. Satyananda Kashyap et al. *Modern Hopfield Networks meet Encoded Neural Representations — Addressing Practical Considerations*. arXiv:2409.16408, 2024. https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.16408
   - Relevant to encoded pattern representations and metastable-state reduction.

4. Yao-Chieh Hu et al. *Nonparametric Modern Hopfield Models*. ICML 2025 / OpenReview.
   - Relevant to one-step retrieval and nonparametric associative-memory formulations.

## Fast/slow decision systems

5. Konstantina Christakopoulou, Shibl Mourad, Maja Matarić. *Agents Thinking Fast and Slow: A Talker-Reasoner Architecture*. arXiv:2410.08328, 2024. https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.08328
   - Relevant to explicit separation of fast proposal and slower reasoning.

6. Zhong-Zhi Li et al. *From System 1 to System 2: A Survey of Reasoning Large Language Models*. arXiv:2502.17419, 2025. https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.17419
   - Survey context only; no architectural claim is imported into TDI-2.1.

7. Zhengkai Lin et al. *Controlling Thinking Speed in Reasoning Models*. arXiv:2507.03704, 2025. https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.03704
   - Relevant to dynamically choosing faster or slower inference paths.

## Interpretation policy

These sources establish that associative retrieval and fast/slow routing are active research areas. They do **not** establish that the TDI-2.1 candidate is novel, superior, biologically faithful, calibrated, or efficient. Those questions remain empirical and are governed by the TDI-2.1 preregistration artifacts.
